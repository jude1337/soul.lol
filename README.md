## What it is

soul.lol is a biolink platform. Pick a username, get a page at `soul.lol/yourname`, and make it yours: colours, backgrounds, fonts, animated effects, live widgets, badges, and more. It's built for people who want their link-in-bio to feel like a real corner of the internet, not another template.

## Highlights

- **Full appearance control** — themes, gradients, background video and audio, custom cursors, text effects, and layout options that go well beyond swapping a colour.
- **Live widgets** — Discord presence, Spotify, GitHub, Valorant, Twitch, and more, embedded directly on your page and kept up to date automatically.
- **Badges** — earn them, show them off, reorder them, or design your own.
- **Templates** — save your look, share it, or apply someone else's in a click.
- **Aliases** — extra usernames that redirect straight to your profile.
- **A Discord bot** that brings your profile into your server, with commands for lookups, presence, and moderation.
- **A native iOS app**, built from the ground up in Swift, for managing your profile on the move.
